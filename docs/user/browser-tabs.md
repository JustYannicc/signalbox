# Browser tabs that follow the thread

In Signalbox Cloud, the tabs you open in a thread's browser panel belong to the
thread, not the device. Open a page on the desktop app and the same thread on
your phone shows it too, on the same tab and scrolled to where you were.
Opening, closing, moving and switching tabs shows up on every device.

Tabs you open are yours. Your agents' tabs show in the same list but stay in
their own browser in the cloud; they never take over a tab on your device.

Each device opens the pages in its own browser. Signalbox never copies cookies,
passwords or anything you typed into a page between devices. Zoom, window size
and back and forward history stay on the device you used them on.

With your 1Password linked, a tab signs itself in when you open it on a device:
username, password and authenticator code, for any site you allowed under
**Settings → Signing in** (or everywhere). It happens only once the tab is in
front of you there, so tabs syncing to your phone never trigger sign-ins or
"is this you?" emails. A site you haven't allowed stays signed out until you
sign in yourself.

On a phone, Google's pages open in the system browser view instead, because
Google doesn't sign anyone in inside an app. 1Password fills it in with one
tap, once per phone. Those tabs follow the thread to the phone, but browsing
in that view doesn't come back to the thread.

- **Desktop:** open the right panel in a thread and choose **Browser**.
- **Phone:** when a thread has tabs, tap the globe at the bottom of the thread.
- **Web:** web browsers can't show other sites inside Signalbox. The panel lists
  the thread's tabs, and picking one switches the tab on your other devices.
  Choose **Open in desktop app** to browse.

Your place in a page comes back on the other device even when the screen is a
different size. If the page changed since, Signalbox scrolls to about the same
spot instead.
