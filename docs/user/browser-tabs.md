# Browser tabs that follow the thread

In Signalbox Cloud, the tabs you open in a thread's browser panel belong to the
thread, not the device. Open a page on the desktop app and the same thread on
your phone shows it too, on the same tab and scrolled to where you were.
Opening, closing, moving and switching tabs shows up on every device.

Each device opens the pages in its own browser, so you sign in to a site once
on each device. Signalbox never copies cookies, passwords or anything you typed
into a page between devices. Zoom, window size and back and forward history stay
on the device you used them on.

- **Desktop:** open the right panel in a thread and choose **Browser**.
- **Phone:** when a thread has tabs, tap the globe at the bottom of the thread.
- **Web:** web browsers can't show other sites inside Signalbox. The panel lists
  the thread's tabs, and picking one switches the tab on your other devices.
  Choose **Open in desktop app** to browse.

Your place in a page comes back on the other device even when the screen is a
different size. If the page changed since, Signalbox scrolls to about the same
spot instead.
